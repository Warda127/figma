Quinoa Vegetable Salad

Diabetes

Ingredients:

Quinoa, spinach, cherry tomatoes, avocado, walnuts, lemon dressing

Preparation:

Cook quinoa, mix with fresh vegetables, drizzle with lemon dressing

Herbed Salmon with Steamed Vegetables

Cardiac

Ingredients:

Fresh salmon, broccoli, carrots, garlic, Provencal herbs

Preparation:

Steam cook for 15 minutes, season with herbs and lemon

Medical Benefits:

  • Omega-3 fatty acids (2.5g/serving)
  • Reduces LDL cholesterol
  • Natural antioxidants
